A community-run Rec Room revival for Quest. Build rooms with the Maker Pen, dress your avatar, and hang out in a world that’s ours.

What you can do here

VR Playground runs on its own private server, so everything below belongs to the community that plays on it.

Make rooms

The Maker Pen works in rooms you create. Build whatever you can imagine, then invite people in.

Shop and dress up

The store is stocked with avatar items, so you can buy, equip and show off your look.

Make it yours

Upload a profile picture, snap photos, and keep your own inventory and tokens.

How to join

You need a Quest headset and an account. It takes about five minutes.

  1. Create your account

    Pick a password on the sign-up page. The server gives you a username, which you’ll see on your account page. You sign in with that username and your password, here and in the game.

  2. Install the game on your Quest

    You need a RecFlare-compatible build of the game. Ask whoever invited you which one to use.

  3. Point the game at VR Playground

    Create a file named recflare.cfg in the game’s folder on the headset, at Android/data/net.recflare.RecFlare/files/, with this inside:

    RecNet NameServer Host = ns.vrplayground.dpdns.org

  4. Sign in and play

    Open the game and sign in with your username and password.

What’s working right now
